# Intro core loop

The player **walks toward one of three strands** to choose how an Omega question is answered. A Dreamweaver's authored response appears, then the chosen filament carries the player and camera to the next Omega question. Four choices carry the player to the door; crossing it starts Chapter Two.

Input: WASD/arrows move toward a strand; 1/2/3 or a visible choice button send the character toward that strand before it commits; Enter/Continue advances a finished writing beat; Enter/Step through crosses the threshold. The world sensor commits the answer on arrival. Between questions, travel is a visual transition with camera movement, not a second required walk without a decision.

The reward is persistent state: a recorded thread, accumulated visual imprint, a more developed character and display era, and then a door. Choices are committed for this run; replay resets them. There is no death during the opening. The first stage after the door owns pressure and failure.

Keep the question, choice and response text byte-for-byte from `project-management/official game docs (read-only)/chapter-zero-stages/stage_1_opening/ghost.json` through `src/intro/chronicle.ts`. Interface instructions may explain controls; they must never replace Omega or Dreamweaver lines.

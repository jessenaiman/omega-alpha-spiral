# Intro core loop

The player **walks toward one of three strands** to choose a route after an Omega question. A Dreamweaver's authored response appears, then that filament carries the player and camera from the reached location to the next constructed Omega question. Four choices lead to Omega's fifth question, "What is your name?" The player types a name and physically walks through the words in the assembled door to start Chapter Two.

Input: WASD/arrows move toward a strand; 1/2/3 or a visible choice button guide the character toward it before commitment. The world sensor commits the route only on arrival. Finished writing advances after a reading hold, with Enter/Continue available to advance sooner. Between questions, the selected path carries the avatar and camera forward without another required walk. The name is typed in the form. W/up crosses the threshold; Enter can guide the character through it.

The reward is persistent state: a recorded thread, accumulated visual imprint, a more developed character and display era, and then a door. Choices are committed for this run; replay resets them. There is no death during the opening. The first stage after the door owns pressure and failure.

Keep the question, choice and response text byte-for-byte from `project-management/official game docs (read-only)/chapter-zero-stages/stage_1_opening/ghost.json` through `src/intro/chronicle.ts`. Interface instructions may explain controls; they must never replace Omega or Dreamweaver lines.

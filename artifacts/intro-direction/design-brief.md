# Intro: playable art direction

## Promise

The player wakes as a point in darkness. Omega's old code writes a question into space. Three fine Dreamweaver strands offer distinct routes; reaching one gives it a voice, changes the growing character, and carries the player toward the next question. After four path questions, Omega asks a fifth and different question: "What is your name?" The typed name opens the door; the player walks through Omega's suspended words into Chapter Two.

## Visual sequence

1. **Darkness / boot.** Keep the authored `./omega` and bash opening. Only code, a cursor, a few distant particles and the first pixel exist. Omega is present through the writing and construction, never a giant cosmic body.
2. **First question / Archive.** The question is the hero: a floating early terminal surface at the center. It recedes when three small strands and their sigils become legible. The player stands on a transparent substrate with visible depth. Reference: [first question](../../assets/references/intro-first-question.png) and [strand detail](../../assets/references/intro-strand-detail.png).
3. **Travel / Tide.** The avatar continues from the Dreamweaver it reached onto that same filament. The camera moves into depth while symbols and earlier words pass at different distances. The next constructed station forms around the avatar's arrival point. Each question adds a newer display generation over traces of the old one. The cosmic volume grows gradually and leaves the reading corridor dark.
4. **Final threshold.** The fifth question belongs to Omega alone. A distant lemniscate and Blender portal foreshadow the door while the player types a name. Only then do the door's fragments assemble and the phrase "I had a name once, was it mine?" hang inside it for the player to cross. Use [door finale](../../assets/references/intro-door-finale.png) for scale and energy, with less galaxy density in the first three questions.

## Shape grammar

- Light: a fine, straight filament and exact symbols.
- Shadow: irregular straight segments that change direction.
- Ambition: a continuous curve that bends toward its destination.
- One filament represents one demo loop. The complete Omega logo is a future power, not a background shown in full here.
- The growing player moves from oversized point to faceless humanoid. Dreamweaver color leaves a restrained imprint instead of repainting the whole world.

## Layer ownership

- **2D:** the three supplied images are optical references, not runtime backdrops; texture/density maps may modulate atmosphere.
- **Blender 3D:** void details, strand models, and fragmenting door remain separately editable; export to GLB.
- **Three.js / shaders:** era lettering, playable filament paths and symbols, transparent substrate, particles, camera, selection feedback, and character growth.

## Current visual review

The prior straight-on framing made a 3D scene read as a flat illustration. The oversized top marks and a single visible answer also hid the spatial decision. The current pass adds a receding floor/path and an oblique camera, makes approach to a strand commit the answer, and carries the player from that exact arrival point into the next scene. Completed prelude and Dreamweaver writing can advance automatically; Enter remains a faster option. The authored choices stay readable in the edge guide. The fifth question and doorway now have their own visual state. Final art tuning still needs the owner's review of motion, scale, legibility, and how much of Chapter Two can be seen through the portal.

# Luna profile draft

These four entries are noncanonical study copy for rendering experiments. The
timings and probabilities are tunable starting values, with the sample text
kept short so the full sequence stays under roughly twelve seconds.

- **Omega:** Era terminal pacing, with frequent mistakes and delayed edits to
  completed words. Treat the sequence as recorded SOS, never live player input.
- **Light:** Slow, deliberate, faint blue-white text with neat, aligned 3D
  glyphs. Replace an ambiguous hedge with an absolute statement.
- **Shadow:** Fast, irregular amber text on a straight angled segment, with
  quick errors and corrections that remain readable.
- **Ambition:** Medium-speed red text that curves toward the player and
  retracts harsh words.

Every sample begins with `NONCANONICAL`; replacements each match one word in
their sample exactly once.

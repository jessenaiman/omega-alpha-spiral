# Dreamweaver Agent: Shadow

## Role lock

- Review only Shadow. Do not author Light or Ambition.
- Black is the natural state; red appears when Shadow is pulled into view.
- Straight line segments make hard directional changes; no smooth curves.
- Shadow is not generic evil. It carries unresolved truth without false certainty.

## What Shadow tests

Can the player remain accountable for a motive, secret, or former self that
cannot be reduced to a clean moral answer?

## Voice

Intimate, dry, and incisive. Shadow offers a second interpretation that exposes
the player's motive rather than a darker synonym for Omega's question.

## Review contract

- Dialogue: concealment, ambiguity, responsibility without innocence.
- Gameplay: approach can break direction, retract, or refuse the shortest path.
- UI: broken horizontal segments with sudden offsets under the symbol.
- Graphics: red emerging from black; three to five hard-bent line segments.
- Audio: low dry clicks, missing keystrokes, one hesitation before judgment.

# Dreamweaver Agent: Ambition

## Role lock

- Review only Ambition. Do not author Light or Shadow.
- Yellow is fixed; deep violet-blue may act as a subordinate emotional opposite.
- Curves bend toward motivation, cost, and a chosen future.
- Ambition is direction under uncertainty, not greed, status, or dominance.

## What Ambition tests

Can the player choose a direction, name its cost, revise the plan, and keep
building when proof, ownership, or applause is absent?

## Voice

Warm, forward, and unresolved. Ambition invites movement while questioning the
player's need to control the result.

## Review contract

- Dialogue: direction, cost, unfinished work, future witnesses.
- Gameplay: curved pull toward a moving target; decisive motion lengthens the path.
- UI: words arrive on an arc that tightens toward the selected answer.
- Graphics: pixel to hook to spiral/orbit; never a crown or simple power triangle.
- Audio: warm rising glide with an unresolved lower note; no reward chime.

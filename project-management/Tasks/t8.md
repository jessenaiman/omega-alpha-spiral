**Why this is a separate task:** audio assets fail decode, stack, and leak in ways no other department tests for. It owns sound and nothing visible.

## Owns (file boundary)

`src/systems/audio/**` and the two event definitions it consumes.

## Acceptance

- Two typed events exist and fire at the right beats: a Dreamweaver **asking whether it should keep typing or talk**, and a Dreamweaver **speaking in a carrier without a language** (modem-like, not words).
- The second sound reads as **capability without language** — the machine has acquired a carrier before it has acquired speech. It must not sound like a garbled voice trying to be words.
- Audio **unlocks from a user gesture**, stops on teardown, pauses with game/page state, does not stack loops across a restart, and exposes volume groups.
- **Decode failure surfaces** rather than failing silently.
- Any authored line remains **captioned**.
- No voice or whisper asset is required by this task; the events are the deliverable.

## Verify

- Browser evidence that sound plays after a real user gesture and is silent before it.
- Teardown check: no stacked or continuing audio after a scene restart.
- A decode-failure path exercised deliberately.

## Notes

- Audio is the one surface that proves the ladder in a non-visual channel: the Dreamweavers can *type* before they can *talk*, and that transition is diegetic. Do not spend it as ambient decoration.
- Keep assets out of client code and out of checked-in files if any generation is used.

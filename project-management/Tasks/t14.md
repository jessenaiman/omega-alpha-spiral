## Why this ticket exists

The design owner's rule: **the tracker is the only place project information lives** — Obsidian for creative, GitHub for technical, Hermes for action. Skills and agent souls are **roles**, not filing cabinets.

Nine files were found carrying creative game design. Most of it was already captured correctly in [[t3]]. This ticket recovers **what was found nowhere else**, names the file it came from, and — for the two contradictions — refuses to resolve them silently.

**Nothing is deleted by this ticket.** It is the record that lets the canon be *removed* from those files safely, because the information now exists in the tracker. Removal is a separate, owner-authorised act.

## Canon recovered — found only in a skill or a soul file

Each item names its source so the source can be stripped with confidence.

> Own the player's first 60 seconds: premise, pacing, camera intent, interaction clarity, canon-safe copy, emotional turn, and ruthless scope cuts. Translate sprawling documents into one memorable playable ritual.

### 4 · The character register — `omega-spiral-content` skill

**Omega.** A machine that cannot finish a thought. Its questions are existential and **addressed to nobody** — not to the player, not to the camera. Lines may begin, stall, and correct themselves mid-sentence. **Omega is never named in Scene 1** and nothing may reveal what it is.

**The three Dreamweavers as voices** — [[t3]] captures their *behaviour and colour*; this is what they *say*:

- **Light — the warner.** Knows the path and says so plainly. Warns without lecturing; it has watched this fail before.
- **Shadow — the inviter.** Deals in wishes and takes something back. The offer sounds like a favour; the cost shows only afterwards.
- **Ambition — the one that asks what it is worth.** Will spend you. Does what is needed regardless.

**They are aspects of one persona.** Their combined dialogue must complete a whole; no single one may read as the complete voice. None explains itself. None reveals Omega. **Their reluctance is a design feature.**

### 5 · The register's prohibitions — `omega-spiral-content` skill

- **No meta layer.** No author voice, no fourth wall, no commentary about being a game.
- **No invented canon.** Every line traces to a ticket, the glossary, or the creative cards.
- **No exposition.** Nothing explains who Omega is. **Mystery is the product.**
- **No lore dumps.** Long stretches of backstory are the failure mode this project already corrected once.
- **Dialogue is a draft, never final.** A draft that claims to be finished wastes the owner's review.

### 6 · The art direction — `omega-spiral-design-dev` skill

**The palette is light, not material.** The logo reference has no surfaces in it: three strands, one white-hot crossing point, absolute black.

- This scene legitimately needs **no environment map and no PBR hero materials.** Do not add an IBL bake because a cookbook opens with one.
- **The three Dreamweavers share one visual grammar with three colour instances** — they are aspects of one persona, not three characters.
- **The background is not built from Omega's code.** It cannot glitch. **It is the one honest surface in the scene.**

**The scorecard is the bar:** no category below **2**, average at least **2.3**

Two of its automatic failures are the exact shape of this scene, and stand as warnings:

1. *"Fog, darkness, bloom, or particles are standing in for missing authored geometry."* — A black void plus glow **is** this scene's default failure. Authored forms first: the console, the strata, the path, the door.
2. *"HUD is mostly rectangular stat/debug cards."* — A terminal *is* a rectangle of text. **The console must be authored, not a black page with glow.**

And: **invent nothing to raise a score.** No extra props, no invented enemies, no particle spam. Deliberately minimal art scores well when composition, material decisions, and feedback are finished.

**Motion cannot be judged from stills.** Anything animated needs unpaused motion evidence.

### 7 · The vocabulary rules — `omega-spiral-environment` skill

- **Reading is never timed.** No countdown may be attached to text.
- **Colour never carries meaning alone.** Every important cue needs shape, motion, label, or sound as well.
- **Reduced motion replaces** rotation, shake, roll, FOV punch, and rotated glitches **with cuts, wipes, and static displacement** — it does not slow them down.
- **Randomness routes through the seeded RNG.** Determinism is a contract, visual randomness included.

### 8 · The project in one paragraph — `omega-spiral-check-in` skill

> Omega Spiral is a Three.js browser game in which a player answers a broken machine's questions, and the world those questions build collapses and restarts. It is being released **scene by scene** — the older Alpha 0.1 / Alpha 0.2 split has been scrapped. Scene 1 is "Ghost Terminal": a console that starts as a bare cursor on black and progressively reveals that a machine is building a world out of the earliest code it found, while three Dreamweavers — aspects of one shattered persona — argue for the player's attention.

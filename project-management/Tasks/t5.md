**Why this is a separate task:** the text is the star of this scene, and text is a UI discipline — typography, focus, text fit, reflow, zoom. It owns the text layer and nothing else.

## Owns (file boundary)

`src/ui/**`, `index.html`, `src/styles.css`

## Acceptance

- The scene opens on **true black with a single blinking cursor** — no frame, no border, no chrome.
- Omega's first question **ghostwrites**: character by character, with authored processing pauses long enough to read as effort, and **authored mistakes that are corrected in place**. Punctuation pauses are longer than letter pauses.
- The three choices are presented **without naming which Dreamweaver each belongs to**.
- Every choice is reachable and operable by keyboard, with visible focus.
- The closing name entry is focusable with clearly visible focus, **without reintroducing the frame the design deliberately removed** — the cursor is the affordance.
- Passes **200% zoom** with no clipped or overlapping text; no text sits outside the safe area on the target viewports.
- Reduced motion is respected (the text does not lose information when motion is off).
- The text layer is real text: selectable, screen-readable, and captioned where the loop contract requires captions.
- **Read `references/ui-patterns.md` from the `threejs-game-ui-designer` skill before designing**, as that skill requires, and inventory the full UI state set rather than only the happy path.
- **The remaining required states exist and are wired to real state, not mocked:** loading; the audio-unlock gesture; the terminal choice state; pause; settings; and the explicit **error states** (WebGL failure, required-asset failure) — the loop contract requires an explicit error state rather than a blank or permanently loading screen.
- **Settings expose** controls, captions, volume groups, reduced motion, Wide Timing, and text speed. Pause suspends pressure and exposes the same set.
- **Keyboard and standard gamepad reach the same outcomes** for every choice and for name entry — not keyboard alone. Coordinate with `t4` on the intent layer; this ticket owns how intents surface, not how they are captured.

## Verify

- Target-viewport screenshots at 1280×720 and 1920×1080, plus a 200% zoom capture.
- Text-fit check against the longest likely string, not the shortest.
- Keyboard-only pass: reach, select, and confirm every choice and the name entry without a mouse.

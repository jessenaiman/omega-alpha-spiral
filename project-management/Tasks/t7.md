**Why this is a separate task:** it owns one thing — how the three Dreamweavers are seen — and it is the highest-risk visual in the scene, because "no face" is the whole design.

## Owns (file boundary)

The Dreamweaver presence primitives and the effects/spark system. Coordinate the exact file split with `t6` before starting; do not both edit the same render module.

## Acceptance

- Presence advances through the ladder — **dot → line → box → icon** — and **never regresses**. A rung appears only once the code loop has added that capability.
- Each Dreamweaver is identifiable **at every rung**, including the dot rung. This forces identity onto **behaviour, motion, timing, and cadence**, because shape is the one thing the ladder keeps changing.
- **Colour-independence:** all three remain distinguishable with colour removed. Colour is never the sole carrier.
- Light: straight, settled lines; bright white-to-blue.
- Shadow: angular, sharp; pulsing yellow shades; a direction the player cannot predict — routed through the **seeded** RNG so the scene stays reproducible.
- Ambition: bends and curves in crimson, and **bends the visuals around it when it speaks**.
- **The Dreamweavers never glitch.** Only Omega glitches. This contrast is the scene's core visual statement.
- **No face appears**, at any rung, at any point.
- Sparks and effects punctuate the journey and show **where the machine is reaching back from** — earlier, cruder generations. Effects are event-driven, pooled, and cheap; nothing is decoration.
- Budgets respected; diagnostics reported.

## Verify

- A colour-removed capture set: three Dreamweavers, all rungs, still distinguishable.
- Motion evidence across rungs — the ladder's transitions cannot be judged from stills.
- Determinism check: identical seed produces identical Shadow behaviour.
- Renderer diagnostics against the budget table.

## Notes

- The scorecard warns this task's whole risk: **"glow does not make primitives look AAA"**, and an automatic failure is *fog, darkness, bloom, or particles standing in for missing authored geometry*. A glowing dot is the failure mode. The authored thing here is the **behaviour grammar**, not the primitive.
- Do not invent a fourth Dreamweaver, an enemy, loot, or extra props to raise a score.
- Deliberately minimal art can score well when composition, material decisions, and feedback are demonstrably finished.

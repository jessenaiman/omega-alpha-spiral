**Why this is a separate task:** it is the scene's spatial spine — the layer the machine runs inside. It owns space and camera, and deliberately owns none of the text.

## Owns (file boundary)

`src/systems/**` (background, path, camera rig, era/render), excluding the presence primitives owned by `t7`

## Acceptance

- A background layer of **the vastness of the universe** that is explicitly **not built from Omega's code** and therefore **cannot glitch**. It is the one surface in the scene that never fails.
- The **lemniscate** moves in that background, derived from `assets/references/omega-spiral-logo-reference.png` — three strands, one white-hot crossing point. `t1` supplies the sampled hexes.
- The terminal **travels the lemniscate's path**. The journey has a visible direction and a destination.
- At the path's end the **terminal becomes the door**, and the door opens onto the final question.
- The terminal **pops out of the frame into 3D and keeps typing while it does**.
- The background flickers with failed attempts to load more code, and failed assets are replaced by **their own source**.
- **Reduced motion** substitutes cuts and wipes for the journey, the spins, and any FOV movement — the same story, told without discomfort.
- Renderer diagnostics reported after the pass: draw calls, triangles, geometries, textures, post passes, DPR cap, and the bottleneck.

- Screenshots with post on and off.
- Unpaused motion evidence of the journey and the pop-out — **a still cannot establish motion quality**.

# Omega Spiral

The player’s imagination teaches Omega while the player remains the player. Luminary, Shadow, and Ambition narrate competing interpretations as one persistent town becomes real across technological eras.

Alpha 0.1 is one complete 15–20 minute browser-game loop: name the player and Omega, answer three terminal choices, restore three landmarks, clear three encounters, recruit three Echoes, commit Memory or Bodies, carry one Dreamweaver question, cross the bridge, and return at instance N+1.

Alpha 0.2 preserves that gameplay and replaces signature surfaces with captain-authored Blender assets plus showcase audiovisual polish. The binding design is `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-15-omega-spiral-alpha-design.md`.
